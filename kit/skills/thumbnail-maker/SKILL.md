# Thumbnail maker

## Sizes
- Reels and Shorts cover: 1080x1920. Keep the important part in the center, since apps crop
  covers in grids.
- YouTube long-form thumbnail: 1280x720.

## Design rules
- One subject (the mascot reacting) plus one object from the story.
- 2-5 words, huge, high contrast. The cover text adds curiosity, it does not repeat the title.
- Same series badge in the same corner every time (brand recognition).
- No clickbait the video doesn't pay off. No other creators' art.

## How
1. Default: an HTML template (one per series) rendered to PNG, with HyperFrames (single frame) or a
   headless browser screenshot. Layers: background art from raw/, mascot pose, text, badge.
2. Or a Flow image (Nano Banana) with empty space for text, then the text added in the template.
3. Make 2 variants. Ask Loy to pick (multiple choice). Save export/cover.png and export/thumb-yt.png.

## Upload notes
- Facebook and Instagram Reels: upload the cover image when posting.
- YouTube Shorts: pick a frame or upload a cover if the option is available on Loy's account.
- YouTube long-form: custom thumbnails need a verified phone number on the channel.
