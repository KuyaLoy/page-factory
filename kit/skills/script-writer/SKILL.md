# Script writer

## Shorts and Reels: 45-60 s, 120-150 words
| Time | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 s | Hook | The strangest true claim plus the strongest visual. No greeting, no "Did you know". |
| 2-10 s | Setup | Where, when, who. The mascot arrives. |
| 10-35 s | Escalation | 2-3 beats: why it happened, what went wrong, the twist. |
| 35-48 s | Payoff | The reveal: "So that's why..." |
| 48-55 s | Button | The mascot's dry one-liner. The last line flows back into the first frame (loop). |

## Writing rules
- One idea per line. Short words. Write for the ear.
- Every 3-5 seconds something changes: a new image, a reaction, a question.
- Use the page's catchphrases from mascot-bible.md, at most twice per video.
- Only confirmed claims. Disputed claims are framed as disputed.
- Caption chunks: 2-4 words each, in the script table.
- One soft call to action at most, at the end ("More shift reports tomorrow").
- Never reuse a competitor's phrasing, structure beat-for-beat, or title.

## Title options (3)
Use a pattern from research/02-trend-radar.md (Title formulas). 3-7 words, concrete nouns,
no false promises. Ask Loy to pick (multiple choice).

## Long-form compilation (optional, YouTube and Facebook)
- 8-12 minutes from 8-12 published episodes of one series.
- Must add new value: a new intro, transitions, new facts between stories and a closing segment.
  YouTube does not monetize "compilations with no original content added".

## Output
brief.md (Script table and 3 title options).
