# Script writer

## Length: 30-90 s, usually 45-60 s
The story sets the length. Never pad a 40-second story to 60. Speaking rate about 2.5 words a second:
| Length | Words | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| 30 s | 75-80 | one sharp fact with a twist |
| 45-60 s | 115-160 | the usual story: setup, twist, payoff |
| 75-90 s | 190-240 | a story with 2-3 real turns. Over 60 s on TikTok may count for its rewards program (check) |

## The 5 beats (scale to the length)
| Share of the video | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| first 2 s | Hook | The strangest true claim plus the strongest visual. No greeting, no "Did you know". |
| to about 18% | Setup | Where, when, who. The character arrives. |
| to about 65% | Escalation | 2-3 beats: why it happened, what went wrong, the twist. |
| to about 88% | Payoff | The reveal: "So that's why..." |
| to the end | Button | A dry one-liner. The last line flows back into the first frame (loop). |
Example at 55 s: hook 0-2, setup 2-10, escalation 10-36, payoff 36-48, button 48-55.

## Writing rules
- One idea per line. Short words. Write for the ear.
- Every 3-5 seconds something changes: a new image, a reaction, a question.
- Use the page's catchphrases from mascot-bible.md, at most twice per video.
- Only confirmed claims. Disputed claims are framed as disputed.
- Caption chunks: 2-4 words each, in the script table.
- One soft call to action at most, at the end ("More shift reports tomorrow").
- Never reuse a competitor's phrasing, structure beat-for-beat, or title.

## Title options (3)
Use a pattern from research/02-trend-radar.md (Title formulas). 3-7 words, concrete nouns,
no false promises. Ask the user to pick (multiple choice).

## Long-form compilation (optional, YouTube and Facebook)
- 8-12 minutes from 8-12 published episodes of one series.
- Must add new value: a new intro, transitions, new facts between stories and a closing segment.
  YouTube does not monetize "compilations with no original content added".

## Output
brief.md (Script table and 3 title options).
