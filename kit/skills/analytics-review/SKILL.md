# Analytics review

## Collect (the user pastes numbers or uploads screenshots)
- Facebook per Reel: views, 3-second views, average watch time, follows, shares. Monetization tab.
- Instagram and Threads: views, shares, follows.
- YouTube per Short: views, viewed vs swiped away, average view duration, subscribers gained.
- TikTok per video: views, average watch time, watched full video %, follows.
Save as pages/<slug>/analytics/YYYY-MM-DD.md with one table per platform.

## Analyse
- Rank videos by hold rate and by follows per 1,000 views.
- Compare series, hook types, length, posting time. Wait for two weeks of data before calling a trend.
- Raise any policy or originality warning from a dashboard first.

## Decide
- 3 keep, 3 change, 3 tests for next week.
- Update topic scores in queue.csv and "What works" in page.md (dated).

## Monetization tracker
- YouTube: subscribers and qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days (goal 10M), or watch hours for
  long-form. From 1 Feb 2027 YouTube says Shorts ad revenue needs 10M qualified Shorts views in the last
  90 days each month.
- Facebook: Content Monetization status (invite-only), Stars (500 followers for 30 days).
- TikTok: what TikTok Studio > Monetization shows for the account.
- When a page starts earning: suggest the paid upgrades in research/03-tools-and-stack.md
  ("Upgrade when you earn"), cheapest first, only where they save real time or lift quality.
