# Analytics review

## Collect (Loy pastes or uploads screenshots)
- Facebook per Reel: views, 3-second views, average watch time, follows, shares. Monetization tab.
- Instagram and Threads: views, shares, follows.
- YouTube per Short: views, viewed vs swiped away, average view duration, subscribers gained.
Save as pages/<slug>/analytics/YYYY-MM-DD.md with a table per platform.

## Analyse
- Rank videos by hold rate (viewed vs swiped, 3-second views / views) and by follows per 1,000 views.
- Compare series, hook types, length, posting time. Two weeks of data before calling a trend.
- Look for policy warnings in the dashboards (originality, reach limits). Raise them first.

## Decide
- 3 keep, 3 change, 3 tests for next week.
- Update topic scores in queue.csv and "What works" in page.md (dated).

## Monetization tracker
- YouTube: subscribers, qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days (goal 10M), watch hours if
  doing long-form. From 1 Feb 2027 YouTube says Shorts ad revenue needs 10M qualified Shorts views
  in the last 90 days each month: track this trend line.
- Facebook: Content Monetization status (invite-only), Stars (500 followers for 30 days).
