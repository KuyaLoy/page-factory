# 01: Monetization and Policy (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, TikTok)

Last checked: 2026-11-05 | Re-check monthly with prompts/trend-refresh.md
Confidence: [High] official Meta / Google page read directly. [Med] 2+ secondary sources or one
careful secondary source citing the official page. [Low] single secondary source, vendor blog or forum.

## TL;DR (most useful if you live outside the US, Canada, UK or Australia)
1. Make every video ourselves (own script, own mascot, own voice, own edit). Both Meta and YouTube
   now reward original work and cut reposts, stitched clips and mass-produced AI slideshows.
2. Post the same video to the Facebook Page, Instagram, Threads and YouTube from day 1.
   Sharing your own content across your own accounts is allowed.
3. YouTube is the clearest money path: public thresholds and a long country list (check yours on Help 7101720).
4. Facebook Content Monetization is still invite-only and Meta publishes no country list. Build
   reach on Facebook, express interest, use Stars from 500 followers.
5. Shorts pay little per view. Plan long-form compilations (8+ min) for YouTube and Facebook
   once a series has 8-12 episodes.

## Facebook

### Content Monetization (the only ads program now)
- One program pays for Reels, longer videos, photos, text posts and Stories, based on performance.
  It replaced In-stream ads, Ads on Reels and the Performance Bonus. [High: about.fb.com 2024-10-02
  and 2026-03-18] The old programs ended 31 Aug 2025. [Med: lilachbullock.com 2026-10-31]
- It is invite-only: "The program is currently invite-only. Invitations are distributed periodically
  to eligible creators." Meta says open enrollment is planned "in the near future". [High:
  creators.facebook.com/tools/facebook-content-monetization, page footer 2026]
- How to ask: Professional Dashboard > Monetization > Content Monetization > express interest.
  The form is not an application and does not guarantee an invite. [Med: lilachbullock 2026-10-31]
- No public follower or view threshold for Content Monetization. No public country list. [Med]
- Meta paid creators "nearly $3 billion in 2025", up 35%. New metrics: Qualified Views, Earnings Rate
  (approximate earnings per 1,000 qualified views), Non-Qualified Views. [High: about.fb.com 2026-03-18]
- Reels must be at least 10 seconds to earn. [Med: lilachbullock]
- Unverified: ARY News (2026-06-21) reported a test where 300,000 views in 28 days could qualify a
  page. No Meta source. [Low]

### Creator Fast Track (not for us right now)
- Guaranteed $100-$450/month (20K-99K followers), $1,000 (100K+), $3,000 (1M+) for 3 months, plus
  instant Content Monetization access. Needs 1,000+ followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube,
  15 eligible reels a month on 10 separate days. [High: creators.facebook.com/creator-fast-track]
- Only for residents of the United States, Canada, United Kingdom or Australia. Creators living
  anywhere else can't join. [High: same page]

### Stars and Subscriptions
- Stars: 500 followers for 30 consecutive days. Subscriptions: 5,000+ followers, age 18+.
  Many countries are on both lists, for example the UAE and the Philippines. [Med: lilachbullock 2026-10-21 citing Meta]

### Originality rules (most important)
- "Content filmed or produced directly by a creator or owner of a Profile or Page is considered
  original content on Facebook." [High: about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook, 2026-03-13]
- Deprioritized as unoriginal: "stitching multiple clips together, or narrating what's already on
  screen" without adding meaning; duplicates or minor edits of another creator's post; low-value
  changes like borders, captions or speed changes. Repeat offenders "may see their account deemed
  non-recommendable and demonetized". Appeals are possible. [High: same]
- July 2025 crackdown: accounts that repeatedly reuse others' content "lose access to Facebook
  monetization programs for a period of time" and get reduced distribution on everything they share.
  Duplicate videos get less distribution. "Avoid using visible third party watermarks." "You can
  share the same content across any of your Pages or profiles." "No more than five hashtags perform
  best." Avoid super short low-value videos. [High: creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content, 2025-07-14]
- Faceless is fine: Meta's rules require you created or meaningfully enhanced the content, not that
  you appear on camera. One vendor reports a 90-day demonetization penalty for limited originality.
  [Low: clippie.ai 2026-10-18]

### AI content on Meta
- AI content is not banned. Meta asks creators to use its AI label for photorealistic video or
  realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created or altered. [Low/Med: clippie.ai citing Meta;
  confirm in Meta Help during setup]

### Your country
- Meta publishes no Content Monetization country list. Your Professional Dashboard > Monetization tab
  shows exactly what your Page can join. Example of history: Facebook launched Ad Breaks for pages in
  the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Jordan in October 2018. [Med: arabianmarketer.ae, communicateonline.me, 2018]

## Instagram and Threads
- Link an Instagram professional account to the Facebook Page in Accounts Center and post Reels to
  both from Meta Business Suite. Threads profiles are created from Instagram.
- Threads API can publish videos: MP4/MOV, H.264 or HEVC, up to 300 s, up to 1 GB, 9:16 recommended,
  250 API posts per 24 hours. [High: developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads, 2025-12 / 2026-06]
- Instagram and Threads do not run a public Reels ads payout comparable to YouTube. Use them for reach
  and followers that feed the Facebook Page and YouTube. [Med]

## TikTok
- Monetization programs, rules and countries change often, and TikTok's help pages couldn't be read by
  our research tools on 2026-11-05. Check TikTok Studio > Monetization for what your account can join,
  and the official Creator Rewards Program page for the current rules. [unverified]
- One vendor page says Creator Rewards needs videos longer than 1 minute. [Low: kineclip.com, 2026-04]
  If that holds, only our 60-90 s videos can count there; shorter ones use TikTok for reach.
- Use TikTok's own AI-generated content label when the visuals are AI.

## YouTube

### Partner Program (official, YouTube Help 72851)
- Ad revenue sharing: "1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months, or
  1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days." Shorts Feed watch
  hours don't count toward the 4,000. Also needed: AdSense, 2-Step Verification, no active strikes,
  live in an available country. Review "typically in about 1 month". [High]
- Check your country on the YPP list (Help 7101720). Example: the UAE is listed. [High]
- Lower tier (fan funding, Shopping): 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 watch
  hours in 12 months or 3M Shorts views in 90 days. [Med: kineclip.com citing YouTube Help, 2026-08]
- Coming change: from 1 Feb 2027 YouTube says Shorts ad and Premium revenue each month needs at least
  10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, and creators must accept updated YPP terms by
  31 Jan 2027. [High: Help 72851 and 12504220] Shorts-only income will need steady volume.

### Shorts revenue
- Creators keep 45% of their allocated Shorts revenue. If a Short uses 1 licensed music track, half of
  the revenue tied to its views goes to the Creator Pool (the rest pays for music); 2 tracks, one third.
  Compilations "with no original content added" are not eligible. [High: Help 12504220]
- Shorts views earn far less than long-form views. No reliable public RPM figure was found; vendor
  pages say roughly an order of magnitude less. [Low]

### Inauthentic content (monetization policy, renamed 15 July 2025)
- Content must "Not be mass-produced, generic, repetitive, or manipulative."
- Not monetizable: "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no
  narrative, commentary, or educational value" and "AI-generated content made with generic or
  unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production."
- Fine: the same intro and outro; "using AI to edit your video scripts or generate a unique background
  visual". "If you use automated tools or templates ... the final product must still demonstrate your
  creative vision." [High: Help 1311392]
- Industry read: unedited AI narration over stock footage is being demonetized; channels with original
  research and a consistent character are breaking out. [Low: outlierkit.com, anijam.ai, 2026]

### AI disclosure (Help 14328491)
- Required only for content that "seems realistic": a real person saying or doing something they didn't,
  altered real events or places, realistic scenes that didn't happen, AI music as the main focus.
- Not required: unrealistic content and animation; "Cloning one's own voice to create voice overs or
  dubs"; AI help with outline, script, thumbnail or title.
- "Disclosing AI content alone won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money."
  Not disclosing when required can lead to labels, removal or YPP suspension. [High]
- A preset AI narrator voice that isn't the creator's own isn't covered in either list. Disclose to be safe.

### Made for kids (Help 9528076)
- Factors include "animated characters or cartoon figures" that appeal to children, but "Animated
  content that appeals to everyone" is listed as general audience. Kids means under 13 in the US. [High]
- Keep topics and tone for a general audience. Avoid nursery themes, toys and kid songs.

### Uploading by API (for automation later)
- videos.insert costs 1 unit in the Video Uploads bucket, default 100 uploads per day. The page says
  "Videos uploaded from unverified API projects are not restricted to private viewing mode."
  [High: developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert, updated 2026-11-03]

## What this means for the plan
- Original production is our moat and our compliance. Keep sources per episode.
- Animate every still and tell a real story. No slideshow look.
- Narrate with your own voice or a clone of your own voice: no YouTube disclosure needed.
- No licensed music in Shorts. Our own or royalty-free tracks.
- YouTube: grow to 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days. Add long-form compilations with new
  narration to reach 4,000 watch hours as a second path.
- Facebook: grow followers, express interest in Content Monetization, Stars at 500 followers.
