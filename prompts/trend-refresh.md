# TREND REFRESH

Run once a month, or when views drop for 2 weeks in a row.

```text
TREND REFRESH.

1. Read ai-handoff-summary.md and page-factory/research/02-trend-radar.md.
2. trend-radar (full mode): what formats and topics are growing now, what is getting saturated,
   and what is getting demonetized or demoted. Date every number.
3. Re-check the monetization rules on the official pages listed in
   page-factory/research/01-monetization-and-policy.md (Meta Content Monetization, YouTube Partner
   Program, YouTube AI disclosure, Meta originality). Note anything that changed.
4. Update research/02-trend-radar.md and research/01-monetization-and-policy.md with the date.
5. Add the 10 best new topics to each page's topics/queue.csv.
6. Update handoff.md and ai-handoff-summary.md. Tell me in 5 lines what changed.
```
