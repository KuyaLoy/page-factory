# WEEKLY REVIEW

Once a week, in a new chat, after the week's last video has been live for 2-3 days.

```text
WEEKLY REVIEW. Page: <page-slug>.

1. Read ai-handoff-summary.md, CLAUDE.md, studio.md, pages/<page>/page.md and analytics/.
2. Ask me to paste the numbers or upload screenshots:
   - Facebook Professional Dashboard per Reel: views, 3-second views, average watch time, follows,
     shares, and the Monetization tab status.
   - Instagram and Threads: views, shares, follows.
   - YouTube Studio per Short: views, viewed vs swiped away, average view duration, subs gained.
   - TikTok Studio per video: views, average watch time, watched full video %, follows.
3. Save it as analytics/YYYY-MM-DD.md. Compare by series, hook type, length and posting time.
4. Give me 3 things to keep, 3 to change and 3 tests for next week. Update topic scores in
   topics/queue.csv and "What works" in page.md.
5. Monetization check per platform (page.md table). If the page earns, suggest paid upgrades that
   save real time, cheapest first.
6. Update handoff.md and ai-handoff-summary.md.
```
