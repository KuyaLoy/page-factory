# WEEKLY REVIEW

Run once a week, after the last video of the week has been live for 2-3 days.

```text
WEEKLY REVIEW. Page: <page-slug>.

1. Read ai-handoff-summary.md, CLAUDE.md, pages/<page>/page.md and the analytics/ folder.
2. Ask me to paste or upload the numbers (screenshots are fine):
   - Facebook Professional Dashboard, per Reel: views, 3-second views, average watch time,
     follows, shares. Monetization tab status.
   - Instagram and Threads: views, shares, follows.
   - YouTube Studio, per Short: views, "viewed vs swiped away", average view duration, subs gained.
3. Save it as analytics/YYYY-MM-DD.md. Compare by series, hook type, length and posting time.
4. Give me: 3 things to keep, 3 to change, 3 tests for next week. Update topic scores in
   topics/queue.csv and the "What works" section of page.md.
5. Monetization check: YouTube YPP progress (subs, Shorts views in the last 90 days),
   Facebook monetization status and Stars eligibility. Flag any originality or policy warning
   shown in the dashboards.
6. Update handoff.md and ai-handoff-summary.md.
```
